Contents claims, valued and evidenced.

Upload the assessor's inventory and photo report. Claimsight builds the asset schedule, attaches every photograph to the right line, researches current Australian replacement costs, and produces an insurer-ready settlement report.

Create an account Sign in

How it works

  1. Create a job with the claim details and policy excess.
  2. Upload the inventory spreadsheet and the photo report PDFs.
  3. Items and photographs are extracted and matched automatically by photo number.
  4. Each item is researched for current AUD replacement cost and depreciated to an indemnity value.
  5. Review, override anything you disagree with, and export the schedule and report.
