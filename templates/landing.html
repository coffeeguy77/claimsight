Questions

Contents claim assessment, answered

How is replacement cost researched?

The item is identified from its inventory description and photographs, then priced against current Australian retailers — the same product where it still exists, or its nearest current equivalent where it doesn't. Every figure is presented with the sources it came from and a confidence rating, so you can verify it before accepting.

Is depreciation applied automatically?

It's calculated for you, not imposed on you. Straight-line over the item's assessed effective life, adjusted for its recorded pre-loss condition and floored at a residual value rather than reaching zero. The age, effective life and rate applied print against every line, and you can change any of them.

Can I override a value?

Any line. Overridden values are marked as assessor-adjusted on the schedule, and the change is recorded in the audit trail with the previous figure, the new figure and who made it. That distinction matters when a settlement is later questioned.

Can I send a report to an insurer?

Print it as an A4 PDF, or publish it to a link that opens in any browser — optionally behind a passcode and an expiry date. Published links serve a frozen copy, so adjusting a figure next week never changes the document already on someone's claim file. You can see when it was opened.

What happens when the AI isn't confident?

It says so rather than guessing. Every line carries a high, medium or low confidence rating, and lines that couldn't be identified are marked unvalued rather than given a number. The schedule shows you where to spend your attention.

Does this replace my field capture app?

No, and it isn't trying to. Capture tools are built for the field and they're good at it. ClaimSight reads the inventory and photo reports they produce and does the valuation work afterwards.
