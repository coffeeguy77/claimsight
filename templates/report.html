Assessment methodology

Replacement values are the cost of acquiring a new equivalent item from an Australian retailer at the date of this report, inclusive of GST. Where the exact model is discontinued, the closest current-production equivalent has been priced and the substitution noted against the item. {% if job.apply_depreciation %} Indemnity values apply straight-line depreciation over the item's assessed effective life, adjusted for recorded condition and subject to a residual floor of 10% of replacement cost. Ages are estimated from model identification, styling and production dates and should be treated as indicative. This claim settles on the indemnity basis. {% else %} This claim settles on a replacement basis, new for old. No depreciation has been applied to any item and no allowance has been made for the age or condition of the items prior to the loss. {% endif %}

Pricing research was performed with AI assistance and reviewed by the assessor named above. Source references are given against each line so that any figure may be independently verified. Assessment text in this schedule is a summary; the full research record, source list and audit trail for every line are retained in ClaimSight and are available on request. Items marked low confidence, or flagged as being of uncertain pre-loss working order, are identified in the schedule and may warrant separate consideration.

This report addresses non-restorable contents only. It does not constitute a determination of policy liability, which remains a matter for the insurer.
